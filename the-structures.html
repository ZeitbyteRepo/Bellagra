The Structures

Four distinct buildings across 163 acres — each with its own purpose.

The Main Cottage

The heart of Bellagra. Recently renovated with Hardie board exterior and a relatively new roof, the cottage sits overlooking the pond and forested grounds. A large east-facing porch captures morning sun while remaining shaded from the afternoon heat.

Living Area 3,669 sq ft
Under Roof Total 7,500 sq ft
Bedroom Suites 2
Full Bathrooms 3
Office Convertible to 3rd BR
Studio / MIL Suite With own bathroom
Exterior Hardie Board
Roof Relatively new

The interior is open-plan — kitchen, dining, and living areas flow together around a fireplace. Windows wrap the living space, bringing in views of the pond and forest. A large office could easily serve as a third bedroom. A separate studio area with its own bathroom functions as a mother-in-law suite or guest quarters.

Standby Generator

The main cottage is served by a full standby generator — less than five years old — running on propane from two 250-gallon tanks. The concrete patios have been painted and sealed in a finish complementary to the exterior.

The Guest House

Located in the northwest corner of the property, this two-story structure is currently operating as an Airbnb and generating rental income. It features its own 20-by-60-foot pool with a surrounding concrete patio and a covered lounge area attached directly to the house.

Living Area 3,773 sq ft
Non-Living Area 1,262 sq ft
Stories 2
Pool 20′ × 60′

Airbnb Performance

The guest house is in its first year of operation and currently averaging one booking per month — a rate controlled intentionally by the owner to limit demand during the testing phase. The property has demonstrated strong activity and could support higher occupancy if a buyer chose to pursue it.

The Studio

A concrete-block-on-slab structure that has been recently, completely renovated — two bedrooms, full bathroom, full kitchen, open floor plan. Includes an enclosed concrete storage unit.

Living Area 1,456 sq ft
Non-Living Area 644 sq ft
Bedrooms 2
Renovation Fully renovated recently

The Equipment Building

A 16-by-30-foot portable building (480 square feet), permanently located on the property, recently renovated with a full bathroom. It sits adjacent to the Belvedere outdoor kitchen area, making it a natural support structure for outdoor entertaining.

Dimensions 16′ × 30′
Area 480 sq ft
Bathroom Full bathroom

  • Standby propane generator at main cottage (less than 5 years old)
